Crystals, chocolate rivers and trees

Two marks for each landscape feature, then the three blended into one park, with three variations of the park. The trees are stylised candy trees, not the game's sprites.

19 · Crystal planet
A cyan and purple crystal cluster bursts up out of the top of the gradient planet.
20 · Crystal geode
The planet is cracked open into a dark cave where crystals grow from the floor, with the flag on top.
21 · Cocoa river planet
A chocolate river winds across the cratered planet beneath the cyan flag.
22 · Cocoa falls
A chocolate river crosses a small planet and spills off its edge into a pool.
23 · Lollipop grove
Two round candy trees stand on top of the planet.
24 · Gumdrop pine
A three-tier gradient pine with cyan candy dots and the flag as its topper, on a purple hill.
25 · Cocoa park
The blend: a chocolate river runs between a crystal cluster and a tree, with the flag on top.
26 · Park tile
A rounded tile seen in perspective, the river winding from the flag at the horizon past crystals and a tree.
27 · Horizon park
Crystals, the flag and a tree stand along a planet horizon while the river flows down its face.
28 · Line park
A minimal line-only park: a circle, two river banks, an outlined crystal cluster, a tree and the flag.
