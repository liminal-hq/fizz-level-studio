What Claude can touch

The studio runs a small MCP server on your machine. Claude reads freely and writes in one of two modes you choose: it proposes changes as a ghost layer you accept, or it edits the level directly with undo.

Ground rules
  • In propose mode, accept and reject are not tools. They exist only in the studio window.
  • A write is a list of script lines, so it can be shown as ghosts, applied, or undone as one step. Direct edits join your undo history.
  • Tile and entity names come from the game's own tables, not a copy that can drift.
  • You can edit while a proposal is open. If your edit overlaps a ghost, Claude is told and the ghost is dropped.
