Using the level editor

Twelve short steps through the prototype: painting, the tiles, the script, working with Claude, the lenses and the windows. Each step has a screenshot with numbered parts, and most have something you can try right here.

The editor itself is on the Prototype page. The screenshots come from it, and the demos on this page are small copies of its behaviour. Claude's ideas in the prototype are three canned ones plus one fixed rule that checks the gaps, and nothing is saved.
