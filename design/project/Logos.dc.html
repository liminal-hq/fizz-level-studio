Six marks for the studio

Each one is shown as an app icon on a dark tile and beside the lowercase name. They were drawn separately, so they differ in idea, not just in colour.

fizz level studio
1 · Rising fizz
A large fizz bubble in the Afterglow gradient with two smaller bubbles lifting off it. The bubble is the series' signature.
fizz level studio
2 · Painted tile grid
A four-by-four grid with ground and a stepped platform painted in, a treat bubble, and a white cursor on an empty cell: a level mid-paint.
fizz level studio
3 · Bubble f monogram
A lowercase f whose crossbar is an orange platform and whose hook lets loose a cyan bubble. The letter becomes a tiny level.
fizz level studio
4 · Builder bracket
Square brackets hold one solid tile with a bubble above it: a level written in the builder language, turned into a tile.
fizz level studio
5 · Crater field marker
A cratered planet disc in the Afterglow gradient with a small pixel flag planted on its rim: a new level being staked out.
fizz level studio
6 · Pixel Ben
Ben in a cap with his soda blaster, on a 16-by-16 pixel grid, firing a square pixel bubble. It looks like a sprite from the tile sheet.
