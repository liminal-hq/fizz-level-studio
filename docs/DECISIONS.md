# Decisions

The decisions taken for Fizz Level Studio by its owner, Scott Morris, each with the reason and the alternative set aside. Where the reason was not recorded beyond the choice itself, the entry says so. An entry that begins **Proposed** is a recommendation awaiting the owner, recorded here so the documents that depend on it can point at one place; it becomes a decision when the owner accepts it, and the word is removed.

## Decisions log

### 2026-10-30 — The Fizz 2 dialect and the rule layer

The owner approved the Fizz 2 plan for level source, the rule layer and the baker. D22 to D31 above are its decisions that touch this studio. In short:

- `.fizz` is a superset of the Fizz 1 script (`script fizz2 0`); Fizz 1 export is a lowering plus a check, with `at=` snapping reported as W104, `chicken` becoming `gloop`, non-standard locks as E120, any `when` as E121 and a solid `floor` as E122.
- Positions: `x=`/`y=` for a cell centre, `at=` for exact, printed by one rule; objects sorted by kind with a required `id=`; the legend includes slopes and reserved Fizz 1 letters.
- The door model is channels, locks and a `when ... then ...` rule layer compiled into 32-byte records at bake time, with no VM on DOS. The baker is `fizz-script` plus `fizz-bake` in the Fizz 2 repository, and the first pack is `LIGHT.FAP`.

**Not decided yet.** The planner's open questions, with its recommendations, are awaiting the owner:

1. A conditional hash step for trigger rules only (recommended yes).
2. Channels and fired-rule bits persisting over a death (recommended yes).
3. W101 silenced by `allow W101` with a comment (recommended yes).
4. The slice gaining the door puzzle (recommended yes).
5. The limits: 32 rules, 32-byte records, two action slots.
6. Captions: shared-table ids only at first.
7. One directory per world, `assets/levels/light/`.
8. Bumping `LEVL` to v2 (recommended).
9. All of the Fizz 1 language parses and prints, only the Fizz 2 subset lowers.
10. The W1xx family, with `bake check` failing on any warning without an `allow`.
11. A lock opens (its channel goes off) and fires only while closed.
12. No delayed actions or repeating timers at first.

The editor's own open questions follow from these: whether it keeps a TypeScript parser or loads `fizz-script` as WebAssembly (and the conformance corpus either way), which letters mean which tiles in the legend, how the editor should refuse a shaft that only a wall-kick could climb, and where the E1xx and W1xx codes beyond those named are assigned.

### 2026-11-04 — The Fizz 2 repository as it stands, and the target model

The studio's documents were checked against the Fizz 2 repository (`main` at `ba1ec8e` and the open pull requests #137, #138, #140 and #142 to #146, and the trial branch `trial/lab-ben-sprites`); [FIZZ2-COVERAGE.md](FIZZ2-COVERAGE.md) has the full comparison. D32 to D41 come from it. The owner also set the direction: Fizz 2 first, Fizz 1 still supported, the two games diverging, Episode 3 built on Fizz 2, and sprites coming soon.

What the Fizz 2 repository now answers of the planner's twelve questions above (provisional where it is an unmerged pull request):

1. A conditional hash step for trigger rules: not answered; nothing but locks runs yet.
2. Channels and fired rules persisting over a death: **answered no**. A death resets the level (owner, 2026-10-30; #140). See D33.
3. W101 with `allow`: not answered; Fizz 2 has no lints yet.
4. The slice's door puzzle: the slice has its two locks as rule records (#140); the start-open-then-close puzzle is not built.
5. The limits: **confirmed** as data: 32 rules and 32 strings of 32 bytes each, one channel action and one cue (an effect and a caption) per rule (#138).
6. Captions: shared captions by id **and** up to 32 level strings (#138).
7. One directory per world: open; `docs/FAP.md` plans `assets/levels/*.fizz`.
8. `LEVL` v2: **not taken**; section version 1 was amended in place because no pack has shipped (#138). See D36.
9. All of the Fizz 1 language parses, only the Fizz 2 subset lowers: unchanged, and refined by D34.
10. The W1xx family and `bake check`: not answered; the baker is planned.
11. A lock opens by turning its channel off and fires only while closed: **confirmed** (#140).
12. No delayed actions or repeating timers at first: the records **have room for both** (`delay`, `time` with `every`, #138); the game refuses every trigger but the lock today. Which are allowed first is the game's choice.

New questions are Q14 to Q22 in `docs/QUESTIONS.md`.

### 2026-11-04 — The owner's answers: states with rules, worlds, scripting and art

The owner answered the open questions of the entry above and set the design for Fizz 2 levels. D42 to D53 record it; D30, D36 and D41 are superseded, D34 and D35 decided, and D38 and D39 amended.

- **What changed in the documents.** The dialect is rebuilt around states, bindings and rules with `lock` as sugar (`LANGUAGE.md`); `LEVL` version 2 replaces the amended version 1; lights are placed objects; the oblique look is the Fizz 2 canvas; the target is fixed per level; world projects (`WORLD.md`) and behaviour scripting (`SCRIPTING.md`) are new; the pixel editor is `EDITOR.md` section 15; the roadmap is reordered with Fizz 2 first (M1 to M8) and Fizz 1 after (M9 to M13).
- **What exists in Fizz 2 today** is unchanged by these decisions and is cited wherever it is used: two lock records, procedural parallax, constant lights, the oblique slice, no wall-kick, one built-in enemy. Every gap between that and the owner's design is a change request in `ENGINE-CHANGES.md` (F9 to F22 are new or rewritten).
- **Still open** are Q11, Q14, Q15, Q17 to Q20, Q22 and the new Q23 to Q35 in `docs/QUESTIONS.md`, the most pressing being what "wall-kick disabled for Fizz 1 levels" means given Fizz 1 has one (Q26).

### 2026-11-04 — The desktop app

The owner set the studio's end goal: a Tauri v2 desktop app to the Liminal HQ standards (D54). The documents had assumed a browser app with a WebAssembly bridge and Playwright. `DESKTOP.md` describes the app from the reference repositories (`folio/AGENTS.md` above all, with Waypoint, Jar, Cadence, `tauri-plugins-workspace` and Fizz Desktop), and D55 to D60 propose what follows for the architecture: Rust owns the domain and links both games natively, the Fizz 1 dialect is ported to Rust with its tests as the corpus, Claude's MCP server is a stdio sidecar relaying to the app, the game crates are pinned git dependencies, and the app is tested with Rust tests and `tauri-mcp-server`. Two facts from the references differ from what was expected: the window tear-off and effects plugins are not published (Waypoint's copies are the source), and there is no shared settings plugin (each app has its own). The new questions are Q36 to Q43 in `docs/QUESTIONS.md`.

### 2026-11-07 — The level model

The owner set the direction for what a level can be and, the same day, answered four of its questions (D73): one tile-map editor for every shape of level, with the continuous picture as the baseline; planes and overlay layers in `LEVL` 2 now, the planes on one level-wide grid and counted in the reserved header bytes 28 to 30 (Q44, Q45); size as a byte budget against the section limit, its numbers to be measured before Studio M2 (Q46); and buildings that fade while caves are cutaways (Q48). [LEVEL-MODEL.md](LEVEL-MODEL.md) has the format layout, the budget, the solvability rules, the editor's part, and the schedule. Still open are Q47 (solvability errors and gates) and Q49 (the data plane), and Q50 to Q52 are new and proposed in `docs/QUESTIONS.md`. The format is requested of the Fizz 2 repository as F26 to F28 in `ENGINE-CHANGES.md`; F24 and F25 stay proposed.
